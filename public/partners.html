THE OPPORTUNITY

From an AI team studio to a company execution layer.

Our initial customer is a solo founder, startup operator or small business owner who needs repeatable research, go-to-market and operational deliverables without immediately building a full department. A self-serve product provides the entry point; coordinated execution, recurring missions, human collaboration and authorized tools form the expansion path.

Commercial model: free entry with plan-based capacity and subscription upgrades when payment configuration is active. We do not claim verified customer traction, collected revenue, investor backing or autonomous external actions.

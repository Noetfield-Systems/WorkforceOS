Get started

  1. Explore the 33-agent marketplace without registering.
  2. Create or sign into your WorkforceOS account.
  3. Open Genesis, review the terms, and explicitly select Join Season Zero.
  4. The first successful opt-in awards 10 internal XP once per workspace.
  5. Open Member XP to claim a daily check-in, take the quiz, build an Agent and share useful feedback.
  6. Create an optional personal referral link, invite one suitable builder and follow their actual first-Agent activation.

Common questions

1. Is the token live?

No. No crypto token has been minted, issued, sold or distributed. There is no official ticker, chain, contract address, exchange or wallet claim.

2. Is Genesis XP real?

Yes, it is a recorded internal participation record in the existing WorkforceOS database. It is non-transferable and has no monetary value.

3. Is an airdrop guaranteed?

No. No future token entitlement or token-conversion formula exists. The tokenomics display is an illustration.

4. Do I need a wallet?

No. A normal WorkforceOS account is enough for Genesis, XP, Studio and referrals.

5. How do I qualify for the first 10 XP?

Sign in and explicitly join Genesis. The membership record and one-time XP award are committed in a single database transaction.

6. Can I join more than once?

A workspace can hold one Season Zero membership. Repeating the join request does not grant more points.

7. How does the leaderboard work?

Only opted-in workspace XP since the Season Zero start is counted. Labels are pseudonyms rather than names or emails. A workspace is not verified as a unique person.

8. What counts as a referral?

Actual new registration with an invite code followed by the new workspace saving its first Agent DNA. A link view or an unactivated account grants no points.

9. What does a referred member get?

Once the first-Agent activation is verified, the newcomer receives a one-time 10 XP welcome gift and the inviter receives 25 XP.

10. Do tasks, likes or reposts automatically create XP?

No. Only published eligible first-party activities generate points. Arbitrary screenshots, claims of work or social impressions are not reward proofs.

11. Can XP pay for a subscription?

No. XP is separate from billing, usage credits, actual paid entitlements and any future creator earnings.

12. Can I send my XP to another workspace?

No. Genesis XP is non-transferable.

13. What does 100M mean?

It is a hypothetical future total-supply scenario, not 100 million minted assets. No issuance, pricing or allocation has occurred.

14. Where are my activities recorded?

In your private account XP history. Public statistics show verified aggregate workspace counts and anonymized standings.

15. Will creating many accounts improve my reward?

Registration abuse is discouraged and duplicates do not increase the same workspace's claim. Current systems cannot independently verify unique persons; no financial airdrop program exists.

16. Can the roadmap change?

Yes. This is an experimental season and token hypothesis. Current earned internal XP entries remain source records; future economic terms require a distinct public announcement.

17. How can I actually help the product?

Test a real Agent DNA, submit a clear product observation, invite a qualified builder, and provide feedback on actual task output.

Public endpoints

GET /api/genesis/program — current season rules and illustrated scenario. GET /api/genesis/overview — real aggregated member/workspace count and XP. GET /api/genesis/leaderboard — pseudonymous standings. GET /api/genesis/me — authenticated account progress. POST /api/genesis/join — authenticated and CSRF-protected opt-in. Existing referrals and XP remain at /api/rewards/.

Rules and transparency

The program is an optional layer of WorkforceOS. No wallet is required, XP has no financial or conversion rights, and access remains possible for visitors without joining Genesis. Read the Whitepaper v0.1 for the full thesis and token scenario.

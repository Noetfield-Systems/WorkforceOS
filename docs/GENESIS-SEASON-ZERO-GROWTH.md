# Genesis Season Zero — Growth & Activation Playbook
Edition 0.1 | 17 November 2026 | Measurable actions, not fake outcomes

## Founder objective
Attract actual builders to WorkforceOS, help them use a specialist AI agent,
and earn voluntary community participation through first-party proof.
Separate this venture from ARQANT, Witness, RLUA and other tokens; preserve
the existing WorkforceOS user database, XP and runtime.

## Evidence-based benchmarks
- Zealy quests: XP, repeat activities, conditional referral claims; do not
  award for mere clicks (https://zealy.io/docs/contributor/invites).
- Galxe points: fine-grained management/qualification
  (https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/9733873-advanced-points-management).
- Virtuals ACP: agent commerce depends on real execution, not just a character
  gallery (https://github.com/Virtual-Protocol/acp-node-v2).
Adopt useful product patterns. Do not copy fictitious traffic or token prices.

## Proposed 14-day launch sprint
1. Publish Genesis, docs, whitepaper, source-backed counters and safe entry
   points from Marketplace, Studio and Rewards.
2. Verify account creation, voluntary opt-in, +10 XP receipt, daily claim,
   first-Agent save, correct quiz, feedback, referrer/new-member activation,
   anonymous restrictions, duplicate claims and leaderboards in CI.
3. Publish a demonstration: choose Echo/Orion/Lyra, customize DNA, save first
   Agent, inspect the actual saved profile; link to the quest that awarded XP.
4. Personal founder outreach (not spam): 20 qualified builder/creator
   introductions, each tailored to one agent use case and a public demo.
5. Invite the first 10 genuine builders; observe first-Agent activation and
   record real improvements. Do not manufacture accounts or buy referrals.
6. Open community feedback intake and a public changelog with evidence.
7. Use referral follow-up only after the invitee demonstrates product intent.
8. At the end of the sprint compare observed activation, meaningful
   first-Agent creation, task use, verified referral activation and feedback.

## Proposed, not achieved, targets
- 100 opted-in workspaces
- 25 actual first Agent DNA creations
- 10 activated referrals
- 20 substantial learning/feedback events
- at least one real independently reviewable task output
KPIs: signup→join, join→first-agent, first-agent→real task,
new inviter→activated referral, weekly retention, cost per activation.
Never call the targets production metrics before source-linked evidence.

## No artificial dependency gates
No chain, token, wallet, upfront funding, extra company or payment account
is required to launch the nonfinancial participation experience. Existing
auth, verified XP and referrals are reused. Anything involving actual
collectible value or an investment offering is a distinct later initiative.

## Member token app (interactive, internal, nonfinancial) — 2026-11-18

Direct route: `/genesis/app`. Anonymous visitors can create an actual WorkforceOS
account or sign in on the same page; an existing session enters the dashboard
without a second account or wallet. Referral query parameters are attributed
to this registration and rewarded only after first-Agent activation.

Daily loop: authenticated claim (+5 XP), streak milestones (+4/+10 XP on
days 3/7), rotating source-validated Specialist Match (+8 XP once per UTC day),
real XP history, opt-in member standing, referral activation and direct link to
Studio and meaningful feedback. No social-click rewards or fictitious balances.

Economy lab: isolated fictional credits and units. Start = 1,000 DEMO credits
and 100 DEMO units (no real assets). Buy/sell at a fixed artificial game rate;
seven-day lock simulator with 0% APY; category allocation simulator with no
returns or monetary rights. Source receipts are recorded separately from XP,
billing and future token issuance. A real token sale, stake or investment is
not represented as live and is not activated by a visual dashboard.

**Benchmark reasons:** Zealy recurrence/leaderboard/referral qualification,
Galxe repeatable daily points with verification, Pixels' rewarding actual play,
not merely registrations. Release measurements focus on activated builders,
daily distinct active members, first-Agent creation and verified referrals.

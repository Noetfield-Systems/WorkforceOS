# WorkforceOS Genesis — Scenario Tokenomics v0.1

Status: **ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY**. This file is a modelling specification, not
an investment offer, a token allocation file, or a contract.

## Candidate model
- Working name: WorkforceOS Genesis Token (concept); no confirmed ticker.
- Hypothetical fixed supply: 100,000,000 scenario units.
- Issued supply: 0. No network or contract selected.
- Community: 30%; Ecosystem/product adoption: 25%; Creators/builders:
  20%; Operational treasury: 15%; Potential liquidity: 10%.
- Inflation, burns, vesting, mint privileges, launch date, utility rights,
  buybacks, sale prices and exchanges: **undecided**.
- No wallet connect, token sale, onchain claim or cash conversion.

## Nonbinding economic rationale
Illustrate which stakeholders could contribute to a sustainable agent
ecosystem. Do not manufacture economic substance by giving fictional
units a dollar price. The proportions are hypotheses to be evaluated
against verified product usage, market benchmarks and available resources.

## Participation-to-future-program separation
Season Zero records existing internal XP after documented activity.
That is the **only** offchain reward being issued.
No XP-to-token exchange rate is specified. Future token eligibility
must be independently designed and announced (if ever), with explicit
contract, rights, exclusions, appeal and anti-abuse rules.

## Considerations for any future issuance
- Economic rights and legal classification should be determined before
  sale or transferable grants; see BCSC and CSA token offering guidance.
- Treat creator compensation and subscription payments as contractual
  operational records, not implicit distributions of community XP.
- Source-backed snapshots and privacy-preserving eligibility checks.
- Multi-party key control, reproducible contracts, test deployments,
  eventual independent security assessment and source proof of ownership.
- Separate seller/issuer from other Noetfield portfolio ventures.
- Never depict a hypothetical liquidity pool or a token supply as live.
